      What is a Mario game?

      In Mario games you will play as the world's most famous plumber on incredible adventures. Immerse yourself in the fantastic world created by Nintendo and discover the immense variety of the many free Mario games. You will be able to play the role of Mario in cult platform games such as Super Mario World, the scenario will be immediately recognizable, Princess Peach has been captured and you will have to travel a huge world full of dangers to succeed in freeing her. Alongside Luigi, Toad or Yoshi you will have to face your old enemies and especially the most famous of them, Bowser! Some Mario games will also allow you to play the many mythical characters of the series, for example in racing games such as Mario Kart. The free Mario games on Snokido will allow you to discover a rich and fantastic universe directly in your browser and without downloading.

      What is the origin of Mario games?

      Before being the main star of many games, Mario made his first appearance in the 1981 arcade game Donkey Kong. This game created by Nintendo was intended to conquer the North American market and Mario at the time was a carpenter and was called JumpMan. With this huge success, the game creator Shigeru Miyamoto decided to make Mario the main character of a revolutionary game, Super Mario Bros., released in 1985 for the Nintendo console and which was the first side-scrolling platform game. The hero Mario had then become a plumber and could explore a world of immense size, unprecedented for the time. Super Mario Bros. become cult and was the first episode in a long saga that would accompany players on every console generation. Mario thus became the icon of Nintendo and we will find this character in dozens of different games.
