    Hyper Knight

    Developer: Boombit   -   7 717 plays

    In the world of the game Hyper Knight, the apocalypse threatens, ancient demonic creatures have reappeared, and their lords seek to spread chaos. The hope of humanity rests in your hands and at the tip of your sword! Don your armor and set out to battle the monsters, collect their souls, and earn gold to purchase upgrades and become more powerful. Eliminate all creatures in a zone to access the next one until you reach the final boss of each region. You can sell souls to the guardian, upgrade your weapons and armor at the forge, obtain familiars that will fight by your side, and enlist mercenaries to accompany you. Your hero can also acquire powers that will allow him to specialize, with skills in melee, fire, ice, or lightning. Hyper Knight offers a very long solo campaign with dozens of regions to explore, as well as a player versus player mode in which you can face other powerful heroes.
